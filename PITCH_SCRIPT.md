## 📽️ Slide Outline

- **Slide 1: Title & Hook**
  - *Title*: Civic Pulse AI
  - *Tagline*: Photo to Resolution in Under 30 Seconds
  - *Visual*: Broken street view split with clean asphalt fix & AI bounding box

- **Slide 2: The Double Tragedy of Municipal Reporting**
  - *Citizen side*: "I tried reporting a broken manhole last year. It asked me for Ward Sub-division ID, Ward Engineer pin, and took 15 minutes. I gave up."
  - *City side*: 1,000 unverified complaints a week. 40% are duplicates, 30% have wrong addresses, and a live dangling electrical wire sits in the same queue as a small garden weed.

- **Slide 3: The Civic Pulse AI Solution**
  - 1 Tap, 1 Photo, 0 Friction.
  - Multi-modal Gemini 3.8 Flash Vision analyzes severity (1-10), size, hazards, and auto-routes to Public Works, Water Board, or Sanitation.
  - Duplicate detection: "3 people reported this already" -> 1-tap upvote boosts priority.

- **Slide 4: The Authority Command Engine**
  - AI Priority Queue: Ranked dynamically by Severity + Upvotes + Proximity to Schools/Hospitals + SLA Days Pending.

- **Slide 5: Proof of Fix & Civic Gamification**
  - Crew uploads fix photo -> AI inspects if the pothole is truly filled flush with road grade (96% Confidence).
  - Citizen confirms fix, collects +100 Civic Points, climbs ward leaderboard, and shares impact card.

- **Slide 6: Business Model & Scalability**
  - SaaS model for smart cities, municipal corporations, and highway concessionaires.
  - Estimated 40% reduction in municipal complaint processing overhead and $64,000+ saved per ward in averted road reconstruction.

## 🎙️ 3-Minute Word-for-Word Pitch Script

> **[0:00 - 0:35] The Hook & Problem**
> "Good morning, judges. Yesterday morning on my way here, I dodged a rim-bending pothole on 80 Feet Road. Two scooters were forced into oncoming traffic. Why didn't anyone report it? Because existing municipal apps make you feel like you're filing corporate taxes. You need to know your ward number, the engineer's circle, upload text paragraphs, and after 10 minutes... your report vanishes into a black hole.
> On the other side, municipal dispatchers receive hundreds of messy, duplicate, unverified complaints. A life-threatening open sewer gets buried under three routine trash bags."

> **[0:35 - 1:25] The Solution: Civic Pulse AI**
> "This is **Civic Pulse AI**. We turn citizen photos into resolved civic issues in under 30 seconds.
> Let me show you our live app right now:
> *[Show Camera screen]* A citizen simply taps the camera button and snaps a photo. That's it.
> Immediately, Gemini 3.8 Flash Vision takes over:
> It classifies the category as **Pothole**, rates hazard severity at **8 out of 10**, estimates the cavity at **1.8 meters by 1.2 meters**, flags that it's right in front of a school bus route, and auto-routes it directly to the Public Works department.
> Notice our smart proximity engine: if someone already reported this 50 meters away, it warns you and lets you upvote in one tap instead of spamming the city queue."

> **[1:25 - 2:10] The Authority Dashboard & AI Routing**
> "Now, let's flip to the **Municipal Authority View**.
> Instead of a chaotic inbox, authorities get an **AI Priority Queue** ranked mathematically:
> *(Severity × 10) + (Upvotes × 3) + School/Hospital Hazard Bonus + Days Pending*.
> High-risk emergencies flash red with an automatic 24-hour SLA countdown.
> Dispatchers can assign rapid crews with one click, drag tickets across our Kanban board, or check our **Predictive Analytics**.
> Look at this insight generated for Ward 12: 'Drainage reports spiked 3.4x — high risk of pre-monsoon road overflow. Recommend preventative desilting.' That is proactive governance."

> **[2:10 - 2:45] Closed-Loop Proof & Gamification**
> "And here is the magic that builds citizen trust: **Before and After AI Audit**.
> When the road crew finishes the repair, they take a photo. Our AI inspects the bitumen compaction, verifies that the hazard is eliminated with a 96% confidence score, and notifies the citizen.
> The citizen confirms the fix, earns 100 Civic Points, unlocks the 'Pothole Patrol' badge, and can share their verified Citizen Impact Card."

> **[2:45 - 3:00] Conclusion & Call to Action**
> "Civic Pulse AI is mobile-first, offline-capable, supports 5 languages, and works for every grandmother with a phone camera. 
> We aren't just filing complaints — we are giving our cities a real-time, self-healing nervous system.
> Thank you, and we welcome your questions!"
